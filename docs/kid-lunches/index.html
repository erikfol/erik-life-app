No kids yet. and each one gets a lunch box on every day.

Click a day to build each kid's lunch box. ✨ Fill empty days packs a suggested lunch for every kid who doesn't have one yet, on the days left this week (skipping days the school is closed). ✎ Box sets what can go in each part. 🏫 School menu shows what the school is serving each day. School days only: Saturday and Sunday aren't shown.

✨ Suggest fills every part you haven't picked yourself, mostly with things they haven't had lately. Press it again for different picks.

School lunch menu

Paste the link to a month of the school's menu from FD MealPlanner (the page with the calendar of school lunches) and press Load. Each day's school lunch then shows on the calendar, just for reference. If the school changes its menu, press ↻ Reload.

Lunch boxes

Each kid has their own box. Write what can go in each part, one item per line. When you build a lunch, each part only offers that kid's items for it. Lunches already saved keep theirs.

Kids

Each kid gets a lunch box on every day. Renaming one keeps their lunches; removing one deletes them.
