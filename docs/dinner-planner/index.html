Your data

Everything is saved automatically. These files are kept up to date in in your erik-life-app folder:

  • meals.csv: every meal with its ingredients, notes, and how often you've had it
  • dinner-history.csv: every day on the calendar with its meal, where you ate out, notes, whether you ate it, and its leftovers
  • db.json: the planner's own data file, which holds everything
